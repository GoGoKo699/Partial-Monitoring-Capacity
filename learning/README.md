Start with the [physical picture](../science/physical_picture.md), then use the
[30-milestone map](../research/LEARNING_MILESTONES.md). Its 12 background targets
assemble the states, records, entropy, coding and energy needed for the task;
its 18 narrative targets connect the capacity proof, qubit measurement optimum,
strict rate separation and optical sister comparison. The milestones retain
their stable identifiers, dependencies, understanding targets and source pointers.

Chapter 10, 2025 revision, arXiv v5**:
[versioned record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.07450v5) and
[author chapter](https://www.preskill.caltech.edu/ph219/chap10_6A_2025.pdf).
The chapter is linked, not redistributed.

measurement record and its true complement; two capacity cuts and a matching
construction; the all-input product-measurement optimum; and the optical
energy limit. Original research ingredients keep their attribution in the
scientific proofs.
